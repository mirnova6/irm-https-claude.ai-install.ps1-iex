    About

    BLS Cockpit is a bilateral-stimulation control panel for trained EMDR clinicians. It is not a medical device and not a self-therapy app — trauma processing belongs inside a therapeutic relationship. Use screen-share (or the client window) to deliver stimulation in telehealth or in person.

    For clinician use

    • BLS Cockpit is a therapist-controlled tool for use within EMDR therapy — it is not a self-therapy app and does not replace clinical judgment.
    • Bilateral stimulation with trauma material should only happen with a trained clinician present, in session.
    • All settings and notes stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
